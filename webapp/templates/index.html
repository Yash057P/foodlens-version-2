The deep-learning core

One CNN, three views, honest confidence

FoodLens uses a fine-tuned EfficientNetB0 convolutional neural network. At inference time it evaluates the original image, a horizontal flip, and a centre crop in one batch, then averages their softmax probabilities.

The deployed model was evaluated on the held-out 25,250-image Food-101 test split: 73.44% Top-1 and 88.21% Top-3. The food details panel is a deterministic lookup for the predicted class, not output generated by the network.

Food-101 is a closed set. Images outside its 101 classes can still receive a prediction. Low-confidence and close-score warnings expose that uncertainty. Nutrition values are educational estimates, not medical advice.

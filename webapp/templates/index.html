About FoodLens

AI-powered food recognition over 101 common dishes. Built with a student-trained EfficientNetB0 model.

Top-1 accuracy 73.9%, Top-3 88.4% over 25k test images (Food-101, Kaggle).

Each scan shows the top 3 candidates, an estimated calorie range, common ingredients and allergen alerts.

Values are approximate and for educational purposes only — not medical advice.
